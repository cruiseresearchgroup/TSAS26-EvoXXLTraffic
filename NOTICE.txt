No new blanket license is granted by this revision. The previously inspected XXLTraffic README states CC BY-NC 4.0 for its dataset and MIT for its code; those statements do not establish the licensing of every artifact in TSAS26-EvoXXLTraffic. In particular, upstream PeMS records and reused forecasting implementations retain their applicable ownership and terms.

Before tagging a completed licensed release, the authors must confirm separate notices for (1) author-owned preprocessing/benchmark code, (2) derived dataset products and metadata, and (3) each reused baseline implementation. This file records the unresolved status; it is not a substitute for a LICENSE or permission from third-party rights holders.
